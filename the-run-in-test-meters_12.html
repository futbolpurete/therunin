The Run In

Pick a tactic & formation before kick-off, then react at a few key moments during the game. Finish outside the bottom three of the table and you're safe.

Full rules

The situation

  • Same club, same run-in as every other player today — the day's scenario is fixed, so it's decisions that separate results, not luck.

Before each match

  • Tactic: Attacking, Neutral or Defensive.
  • Formation: 4-3-3 (attack-leaning), 4-4-2 (balanced) or 5-3-2 (defence-leaning). The two stack.

During each match

  • At a few key moments you can switch tactic (max 2 per game) and make a substitution (max 5 per game): Fresh Legs, Go Attacking, or Shore It Up.

Staying up

  • You're one of a group of clubs fighting the drop. Finish outside the bottom three of the table and you're safe; if the arithmetic rules you out before the run ends, it's over immediately.

Manager's Ledger

Your managerial career so far — a different club, and a different country, most days.

    This page explains the cookies and similar storage technologies used on therunin.app, what they're for, and how you can control them.

    1. Cookies vs. local storage

    A "cookie" is a small file a website asks your browser to store, usually sent back to the site on later visits. "Local storage" is a similar browser feature, but — unlike a cookie — it is never automatically sent over the network; it just sits in your browser until a site reads it, and it's only accessible to the site that created it. We call out the distinction because The Run In's own game data uses local storage rather than cookies — section 3 below covers the separate cookies Google sets here for advertising.

    2. What we currently store

    The Run In doesn't currently set any cookies. It stores one item of local storage on your device, purely to run the game:

    dailyRelegation.v1 Remembers your daily result, stats, and Manager's Ledger history so progress carries over between visits. First-party local storage, essential to the game — never sent to our servers. Until you clear it via your browser settings

You can control ad cookies through your browser settings and the opt-outs listed in section 4 below, and we'll update this page and the table above if the details change.

    You're always in control of cookies and local storage through your own browser:

    • Most browsers let you view, block, or delete cookies and site data for individual sites via their privacy/security settings.

    • You can opt out of personalised advertising from Google specifically at adssettings.google.com, and from participating companies generally via youronlinechoices.com.
