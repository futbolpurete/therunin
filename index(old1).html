Full rules

The situation

  • Same club, same run-in as every other player today — the day's scenario is fixed, so it's decisions that separate results, not luck.

Before each match

  • Tactic: Attacking, Neutral or Defensive.
  • Formation: 4-3-3 (attack-leaning), 4-4-2 (balanced) or 5-3-2 (defence-leaning). The two stack.

During each match

  • At a few key moments you can switch tactic (max 2 per game) and make a substitution (max 5 per game): Fresh Legs, Go Attacking, or Shore It Up.

Staying up

  • You're watching one benchmark rival. Finish level with them or ahead and you're safe; if the arithmetic rules you out before the run ends, it's over immediately.
